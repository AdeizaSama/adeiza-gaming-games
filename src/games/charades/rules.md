Play in teams. On your team's turn, one player gives the clues: they hold the phone so only they can see the card, and get their team to say what's on it before time runs out.

## A turn

1. Pass the phone to the clue-giver, who taps **Start**.
2. Give clues for the card, without saying any word on it.
3. Tap **Got it** when your team says it, or **Skip** to move on to the next card.
4. When time runs out, check the cards together. If a tap was wrong, tap **Adjust**, tap the card to fix it, then tap **Done**.

Every team plays once a round. After the last round, the team with the most points wins.

## Modes

- **Describe:** say anything but the words on the card.
- **Mix it up:** each card says how to clue it. **Describe it**, **act it out** without words, or **sing or hum it**.

## Points

Unless you change them in Advanced settings:

- A card your team gets is worth **2 points** described, or **3 points** acted or sung.
- A skip costs **1 point**.
- A turn never scores below 0.
- The card on screen when time runs out doesn't count. If your team said it just in time, count it in review.

## Tips

- Take turns giving clues, so everyone gets a go.
- Stuck on a card? A quick skip often scores more than a long struggle.

Learn words in pairs, alone or in teams. Pick Free Play on the setup screen to play on your own, at your own pace.

## Flip cards

1. Read the card. Say its partner out loud, or in your head.
2. Tap **Show the answer**.
3. Be honest: tap **Knew it** or **Not yet**.

## Match-up

1. Three words are at the top, and ten tiles below.
2. Tap the three tiles that are their partners, in any order, then **Check**.
3. Right tiles turn green, wrong ones red, and ones you missed are outlined.

## Odd one out

1. A group is at the top ("les animaux"), and ten words below.
2. Seven are in the group. Tap the three that don't belong, then **Check**.

## Points

- **1 point** for every card you knew, or every right tile.
- In teams, every team plays once a round. After the last round, the team with the most points wins.

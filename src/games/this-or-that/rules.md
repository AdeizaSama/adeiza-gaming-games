Play in teams. On your team's turn, you hold the phone: it shows a word and two answers to pick from. Pick the right one before time runs out.

## A turn

1. Hand the phone to the team named on screen, who taps **Start**.
2. Read the word, and tap one of the two answers. You only get one tap.
3. The phone shows whether you were right, and the right answer if not.
4. Tap **Next question** for the next word, or **End turn** after the last.

A turn is ten words in 60 seconds, unless you change it on setup. Every team plays once a round. After the last round, the team with the most points wins.

## Ways to play

- **Ten words:** each turn is ten words. The turn ends when they're answered, or when time runs out.
- **Against the clock:** answer as many as you can in 60 seconds. The next word comes up by itself.

## Points

- **1 point** for every right answer, unless you change it on setup.
- A wrong answer costs nothing, so always pick one.

## Tips

- The two answers are always in the same place, so you can answer fast once you know.
- The small line under each word is what it means.

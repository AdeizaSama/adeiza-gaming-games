Play in teams. On your team's turn, you hold the phone: it shows a question and a few answers to pick from. Pick the right one before time runs out.

## A turn

1. Hand the phone to the team named on screen, who taps **Start**.
2. Read the question out, talk it over, and tap the answer you agree on. You only get one tap.
3. The phone shows whether you were right, and the right answer if not.
4. Tap **Next question** for the next one, or **End turn** after the last.

Every team plays once a round. After the last round, the team with the most points wins.

## Ways to play

- **Set questions:** each turn is a set number of questions, one unless you change it. The turn ends when they're answered, or when time runs out.
- **Against the clock:** answer as many as you can before time runs out.

## Points

- **1 point** for every right answer, unless you change it on setup.
- A wrong answer costs nothing, so always pick one.

## Tips

- Agree before you tap: one tap is final.
- Against the clock, don't argue too long. A quick guess beats no answer.
